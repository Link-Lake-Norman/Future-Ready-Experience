Future Ready™ Lesson Builder

This update was built against the uploaded future-ready-v2 project.

Adds:
- /ambassador/lesson-builder
- Admin-controlled facilitator Lesson Builder permissions
- Protected Future Ready™ master curriculum
- Ambassador/cohort lesson customization
- Activities, role play, improv, case scenarios, reflections, timing, materials, speakers, and resources
- Draft, approval, and publish workflow
- Published school version displayed on the weekly curriculum page
- Lesson Builder link in the Ambassador navigation

Install:
cd ~/future-ready-v2
unzip -o ~/Downloads/Future-Ready-Lesson-Builder.zip -d ~/future-ready-v2
npx tsc --noEmit

Open permissions:
http://localhost:3000/ambassador/admin/permissions

Open Lesson Builder:
http://localhost:3000/ambassador/lesson-builder
